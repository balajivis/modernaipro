# Compensation Policy
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2024

## Overview
This policy outlines how the company determines, manages, and reviews employee compensation to ensure fairness, competitiveness, and alignment with business performance.

## Compensation Philosophy

Our company is committed to:
- **Market competitiveness:** Salaries competitive with industry standards for similar roles
- **Internal equity:** Fair compensation regardless of gender, race, or other protected characteristics
- **Performance alignment:** Compensation reflects contribution and results
- **Transparency:** Clear criteria and processes for salary decisions

## Salary Determination

### New Hires
1. **Market analysis:** HR reviews market rate for role/level/location
2. **Benchmarking:** Uses salary surveys (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Radford, etc.)
3. **Internal equity:** Reviews compensation of similar-level employees
4. **Offer:** Based on experience, skills, and market rate
5. **Negotiation:** Hiring manager may negotiate within approved band

### Salary Adjustments

#### Cost of Living
- Annual review of all salaries based on inflation/COL
- Minimum 2% adjustment for inflation
- May be higher based on company performance

#### Market Adjustments
- If market rate for role increases, salary adjusted upward
- Conducted during annual compensation review cycle
- Priority to highest performers and longest tenured

#### Promotion
- Role change: 10-20% increase within new role's band
- Lateral move: No mandatory increase; performance-based
- Manager promotion: 15-25% increase

## Bonus Program

### Eligibility
- Full-time employees after 90-day probation period
- Part-time employees: Pro-rated bonus
- Contractors: Not eligible

### Structure
- **Performance-based:** Tied to individual and company goals
- **Paid annually:** Typically in Q1 following year-end
- **Discretionary:** Company reserves right to modify based on performance

### Bonus Calculation
```
Bonus = Base Salary × Target Percentage × Achievement Multiplier
```

Example:
- Base: $100,000
- Target: 20% ($20,000)
- Achievement: 100% (all goals met)
- **Bonus: $20,000**

### Achievement Multiplier
- Below 50% of goals: 0% bonus (no payout)
- 50-75% achievement: 50% of target bonus
- 75-100% achievement: 100% of target bonus
- 100%+ achievement: 100-150% of target bonus (stretch bonus)

### Goals
- Individual goals: 50% of bonus calculation
- Team/Department goals: 30% of bonus calculation
- Company goals: 20% of bonus calculation

## Stock Options and Equity

### Eligibility
- Employees at Mid-level and above typically receive stock grants
- Vesting schedule: 4-year vest with 1-year cliff
- Annual refresh: Additional grants for strong performers

### Vesting
- **Cliff:** 25% vests after 1 year (cliff period)
- **Monthly:** Remaining 75% vests monthly over remaining 3 years
- **Acceleration:** Upon promotion, some acceleration may occur

## Benefits Value

### Benefits Package Value (approximate)
- Health Insurance: $12,000-18,000/year
- 401k Match: 4-8% of salary
- PTO: 15-25 days/year (valued at salary equivalent)
- Stock/Bonus: Variable
- **Total package typically 20-30% above base salary**

## Annual Compensation Review

### Timeline
- Reviews conducted January-February each year
- Decisions communicated by end of February
- Increases effective March 1

### Process
1. **Manager reviews:** Completes performance assessment
2. **Equity review:** HR analyzes for pay equity issues
3. **Budget review:** Finance approves total increase pool
4. **Communication:** Manager discusses with employee
5. **Documentation:** Changes entered in payroll system

### Frequency of Increases
- Annual review: Minimum 2%, up to 10% based on performance
- Off-cycle review: May occur for promotions, market adjustments
- Underperformance: May result in no increase or decrease (rare)

## Special Compensation Situations

### Executive Compensation
- CEO and executives follow separate compensation structure
- Approved by Board Compensation Committee
- Performance metrics tied to business objectives

### Remote Work Salary Adjustment
- No penalty for remote work in same metro area
- Different metro area: May adjust based on local market
- International: Subject to local market rates

### Relocation
- Salary may be adjusted if employee relocates to significantly different market
- Changes not punitive; based on market analysis
- Discussed before acceptance of new location

## Pay Equity and Transparency

### Annual Equity Audit
- HR conducts pay equity analysis by role, level, department, gender, race
- Reports to executive leadership
- Corrective actions for identified disparities

### Transparency
- Salary bands published to employees
- Pay band for role is not secret
- Employee may request their band information

### Dispute Resolution
- Employee may challenge salary decision
- Process: Discuss with manager → HR review → Skip-level manager review
- Final decision by HR director or VP
