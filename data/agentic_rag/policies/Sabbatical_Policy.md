# Sabbatical Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2023
**Last Updated:** June 1, 2023

## Overview
The company recognizes the value of extended time off for employee renewal, professional development, and personal growth. The sabbatical program provides eligible employees with the opportunity to take extended leave while maintaining their employment and benefits.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Core Requirements
- **Minimum tenure:** 5 years of continuous employment
- **Employment status:** Must be active, full-time employee
- **Performance:** No performance improvement plan (PIP) active or completed in past 12 months
- **Disciplinary status:** No active disciplinary actions or warnings in past 12 months
- **Clearance:** Written approval from department head and HR

### Department Specific
- Engineering: Director+ approval required
- Sales: VP+ approval required
- Marketing: Director+ approval required
- Support roles: Manager approval required

## Sabbatical Duration

### Permitted Lengths
- **Standard:** 1-3 months unpaid leave
- **Extended:** Up to 6 months (requires C-level approval)
- **Academic:** Extended sabbatical for graduate education (negotiated case-by-case)

### Frequency
- Employees may take one sabbatical per 5-year tenure period
- Example: An employee hired 5 years ago is eligible for one sabbatical; at 10 years they're eligible for a second

## Before Sabbatical

### Preparation Phase (90 days before start)
1. **Propose sabbatical:** Submit request to manager with:
   - Proposed duration (start and end dates)
   - Reason for sabbatical (rest, education, travel, family time, etc.)
   - Coverage plan (who will cover responsibilities)
   - Knowledge transfer plan

2. **Coverage planning:** Work with manager to:
   - Identify who will assume employee's responsibilities
   - Document key processes and contacts
   - Create handoff materials
   - Establish timeline for knowledge transfer

3. **Approvals:** Obtain written approval from:
   - Direct manager
   - Department head
   - HR director
   - Finance (for budget impact)

4. **Legal review:** HR will conduct:
   - FMLA eligibility check
   - Benefits continuation review
   - Compliance verification

## During Sabbatical

### Employment Status
- Employee remains on payroll for benefits only (unpaid leave)
- Benefits continue: health insurance, 401k employer match, life insurance
- Employee ID and system access maintained for emergency contact purposes
- No work communication expected; employee should not respond to work emails

### Benefits
- Health insurance premium: Employee pays 100% of premium during leave
- 401k contributions: Suspended (no employer match during unpaid leave)
- Accrual: PTO and benefits do not accrue during unpaid sabbatical
- Stock options: Vesting continues as normal

### Communication
- Employee should designate emergency contact point
- Return date is fixed (no indefinite leave)
- Early return negotiated with manager if circumstances change

## After Sabbatical

### Return to Work
- Employee returns on agreed-upon date
- Same position held if available; similar role if not
- Salary and benefits resume at previous level
- 1-week transition/ramp-up expected

### Debrief
- Manager conducts return meeting
- Employee may briefly share sabbatical experience
- No performance penalty for taking sabbatical

## Special Cases

### Sabbatical + Parental Leave
- Sabbatical may be combined with FMLA-protected parental leave
- Total protected leave subject to FMLA limitations (12 weeks paid/unpaid)
- Request must specify breakdown of parental leave vs. personal sabbatical

### Education Sabbatical
- For pursuing degrees or professional certifications
- May qualify for tuition reimbursement if course relates to job
- Employee must commit to 2 years employment after return (or repay tuition)

### International Sabbatical
- Employee must notify HR of travel destination
- Tax implications reviewed with Finance
- Travel insurance and emergency contacts documented

## Denial and Reconsideration

### Reasons for Denial
- Insufficient tenure
- Active performance or discipline issues
- Business criticality (temporary; may reapply later)
- Inadequate coverage plan

### Appeal Process
- Employee may request review from HR director
- VP review available for further appeal
- Final decision by VP HR or CFO

## Post-Sabbatical Obligations

### Minimum Tenure
- Employee must work at least **2 more years** after sabbatical to avoid repayment
- If separation occurs within 2 years, may owe back prorated costs (benefits continuation)

### Non-Compete Waiver
- Sabbatical considered part of total tenure
- Non-compete agreements remain in effect even during unpaid leave

## Financial Impact

### Cost Responsibility
- Unpaid leave: No salary cost to company
- Benefits: Company continues health insurance subsidy (employee pays premium)
- Back-fill: Temporary/contract staff or internal coverage

### Department Budget
- Hiring manager responsible for coverage plan costs
- HR reviews financial impact during approval process
