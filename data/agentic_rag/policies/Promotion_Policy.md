# Promotion Policy
**Version:** 1.5
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2024

## Overview
This policy outlines the company's approach to career progression and promotion within the organization. Promotions recognize demonstrated performance, expanded responsibilities, and contribution to company goals.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Minimum Requirements
1. **Time in current role:** Minimum 1 year of tenure in current position
2. **Performance rating:** Most recent review rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher
3. **Leadership rating:** If promoting to management, "Exceeds Expectations" minimum
4. **Attendance:** No active disciplinary actions or warnings in past 12 months
5. **Skills alignment:** Demonstrates proficiency in skills required for new role

### Career Level Progression
```
Entry (0-2 years)    → Mid-level (2-5 years)
Mid-level (2-5 yrs)  → Senior (5-8 years)
Senior (5-8 years)   → Staff/Lead (8+ years)
Individual contributor → Manager/Team Lead
```

## Promotion Types

### Vertical Promotion
- Move from one level to higher level within same function
- Example: Engineer → Senior Engineer → Staff Engineer
- Salary increase: 10-20% based on market and performance

### Lateral Promotion
- Move to different department/function at same level
- No salary increase required
- Used for skill development or career exploration

### Managerial Promotion
- Move from individual contributor to people manager
- Requires demonstrated leadership capability and technical excellence
- Leadership training required within 90 days
- Salary increase: 15-25%

## Promotion Process

### Nomination and Planning (Months 1-2)
1. **Manager nominates candidate** with:
   - Current role and performance summary
   - Target role and justification
   - Competency gap analysis
   - Development plan to address gaps

2. **HR reviews nomination:**
   - Verifies eligibility (1-year tenure, performance rating, etc.)
   - Confirms role availability or timeline
   - Provides feedback to manager

### Development Phase (Months 3-6)
1. **Competency development:**
   - Employee works on specific skills needed for new role
   - Takes relevant training courses
   - Applies skills in current role
   - Manager provides coaching and feedback

2. **Expanded responsibility:**
   - Employee takes on projects related to new role
   - Cross-functional collaboration increases
   - Leadership opportunities created

### Evaluation Phase (Month 6-7)
1. **360-degree feedback:**
   - Peers assess readiness
   - Customers/clients evaluate impact
   - Manager provides detailed feedback
   - Skip-level manager provides perspective

2. **Manager assessment:**
   - Written evaluation of readiness
   - Business need assessment
   - Budget approval (if applicable)

3. **HR review:**
   - Approves or recommends adjustments
   - Finalizes compensation package
   - Schedules promotion effective date

### Approval

## After Promotion

### First 90 Days
- **Ramp-up period:** Adjust to new responsibilities
- **Check-ins:** Bi-weekly manager meetings
- **Learning:** Access to training for new role
- **Performance expectations:** Gradually increase to full role requirements

### Salary Review
- New salary effective on promotion date
- Increase incorporated into paycheck
- Benefits unchanged (unless manager role triggers eligibility changes)
- Bonus percentage may increase with level

### Responsibilities
- Employee expected to perform all duties of new role
- Previous role transitioned (backfill/redistribution)
- Mentor other team members if applicable

## Denial and Appeal

### Reasons for Denial
- Does not meet minimum eligibility criteria
- Performance rating below threshold
- Role not currently available (may be reconsidered later)
- Competency gaps not adequately addressed
- Business circumstances changed (hiring freeze, etc.)

### Appeal Process
1. Manager discusses denial with employee (feedback meeting)
2. Employee may request review from HR director
3. HR director meets with skip-level manager for second perspective
4. Final decision communicated within 1 week
5. Alternative development plan offered

## Performance Management Post-Promotion

### First Review in New Role
- Conducted at 6-month mark
- Assesses how well employee is performing new role
- Sets expectations for remainder of first year
- Identifies continued development needs

### Underperformance
- If employee struggles in new role, manager coaches immediately
- May result in role change or performance improvement plan
- Demotion possible if underperformance is significant
- Demotion handled with dignity; previous level role offered if available

## Criteria for Promotion Denial Due to Performance

**Do not promote if:**
- Last performance rating was "Below Expectations" or "Needs Improvement"
- Active performance improvement plan
- Significant disciplinary action in past 12 months
- Failed to demonstrate key competencies in assessment

## Equity and Consistency

### Pay Equity
- Promotions reviewed for pay equity impact
- Women and underrepresented groups tracked for promotion rates
- Ensure promotions not systematically lower than peers

### Talent Pipeline
- Company maintains succession plan for key roles
- High-potential employees identified and developed
- Career pathing discussed annually in 1-on-1s
